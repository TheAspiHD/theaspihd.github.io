Berlin Trip - Chronologically sorted.

Suffering. Ze Doitsche Bah(n) was like "nuh UH you shalst NOT travel without issues"

Reistag! erm I mean what-

20.10.2026 // Tuesday

Very interesting day:

Science Museum! We went there VERY early in the morning. It was like a museum day so all entry prices were 0,00€.
Tho all the little kids and the daycare groups that went there made me go insane. It was so damn loud in the whole museum. Unbearable.
Later on at 11:40 AM I went to the debt clock. 11:41:45 on that day germany owed 2.821.394.692.220€ Thats 2.821 TRILLION EUROS. 10 Seconds later its about 100k more.
After that I went to the Charité, walked past the Ministery for Technology, went to checkpoint Charlie and then back to the Hotel.

21.10.2026 // Wednesday

Another museum: Technology this time.
I saw the first computer, old trains, even some from 1933-1945 with a cross with lets say arm extensions rotated by 90 degrees.
After that "experience" I visited another place that has leftovers from the dark age!

The Olympia-Stadium: Usain bolt set his world record here. And I saw a bell with the same symbol that the train has. The stadium is massive. Really.
It is out of like sandstone. Hitler himself ordered the construction and wanted it to be a better version of the colosseum. (->reference) I must say, yes. It certainly is massive and has a colosseum-type feeling.
At least there is a progress flag on top of it nowdays.
In the evening I went to the hotel I stayed at last time. Was a nice trip to Berlin-Grunewald.

22.10.2026 // Thursday

The last day was very uneventful. Checking out, getting to the train, going home like normal... Nope! I WAS STUCK AT A SMALL TRAIN STATION STANDING THERE FOR WHAT FELT LIKE 6 FRICKIN LIFETIMES! Basically what happened: I got out at a station and then DB was like "hmm nah train is gna arrive at 16:15 trust sis". It kept gradually going up until the train arrived at 16:30. It was supposed to arrive a bit prior to 4pm by the way. I had to travel 1 station with that train before changing. And uhhh lets just say, that went chaotic. The train had to stop in a tiny village that usually only gets attention by regional trains and not IC/ICE's. "Yea so because a track is broken and there is only one-track-travel right now, we have to wait for a train with 85 minutes of delay to pass us. We have to wait about 30-35 minutes." When I heard that, I was like "we deadass rn, gng?" Sadly, the train operator was dead serious. After what felt like way longer than 30 Minutes we got going again. With over an hour of delay I arrived. Worst part is: That train is a known traveller train for people wanting to go to Islands. And the ferry depends on the Water level. They had to stay the night in a hotel and get the ferry the next morning. Real frickin nice, DB. In the end I arrived at 6:30pm at home. The "Taxi-Driver" waiting in my home town sure was mad.
